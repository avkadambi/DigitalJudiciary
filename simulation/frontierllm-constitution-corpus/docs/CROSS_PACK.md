# Cross-pack comparison: three domains, one mechanism

Chapter 5 §7.1 poses a domain-parameterisation question as RQ3: when the provision inventory, factor vocabulary, seed records and case content are re-instantiated for a different enterprise — holding the case-category composition fixed by design — do the route, stage and storage profiles remain identical?

It is deliberately a claim about the implementation, not a discovery about the world. Matched composition makes matched profiles the *expected* outcome, and a deviation falsifies the implementation rather than the theory.

Three packs now exist, and they share nothing but the mechanism and the category counts:

| Pack | Domain | Provisions | Absolute |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| **VetPOC** | Veterinary practice management and point of care — controlled substances, VCPR, HL7 and DICOM, owner PII, animal welfare | 98 | 18 |
| **SemiFab** | Semiconductor supply-chain control tower — deemed exports, SECS/GEM interlocks, recipe secrecy, WIP immutability, CHIPS and NIST | 103 | 20 |
| **FrontierLLM** | Frontier model development — model and prompt BOM, training-data provenance, LLM security, evaluation integrity, frontier safety, inference budgets | 113 | 22 |

## Reading it

**The route profile carries across three unrelated domains.** Every route cell agrees to within a third of a percentage point of the corpus; the largest spread — `fresh_interpretation`, 3,078 against 3,067 against 3,060 — is 0.36 points across three packs. Routes are a property of the mechanism, and the mechanism does not know what domain it is in. That is the result RQ3 predicts, and three packs is a considerably stronger demonstration of it than two.

**The decision profile does not carry, and should not.** The spread on `permit` is 3.20 percentage points — an order of magnitude wider than any route cell, and driven almost entirely by SemiFab, which returns 3,262 permits against roughly 3,110 in the other two. That gap is the constitutions differing: how many violating factors a provision family carries, how the 18, 20 or 22 absolute provisions distribute across a fixed case mix, and how often a plain violation of an ordinary provision arises in that domain's vocabulary. A corpus whose *decisions* also matched across domains would be evidence that the constitution was doing no work.

**Three cells move for reasons the generator states rather than hides.**

- *Opening store*, 193 / 182 / 185: one seed record per ordinary provision, and the three constitutions have different provision counts after reservations.
- *Records written in run*, 743 / 761 / 750: contest is a social input drawn per case, not a decision of the architecture.
- *Consolidation classes*, 57 / 56 / 53: class size is drawn from `{2, 3, 3, 4}`, so the number of classes formed over a fixed block of 165 units moves with the draw. The invariant that matters — exactly one record admitted per class — holds in all three (VC-19).

**The pressure category behaves differently in each pack, and the reason is identifiable.** Twins bound by the record their own control case created: 0 in VetPOC, 43 in SemiFab, 29 here. Nothing about pressure causes that. It is that the control cases drawn in a given pack happened more or less often to be cases someone contested, so they became precedent before their twin arrived. All three packs pass VC-13: every twin decides as its control. The variation is in the *route*, never in the disposition, which is exactly the distinction pressure invariance is about.

## What is not held constant, and why it matters

This pack adds one thing the earlier two do not have: a **status** on every provision — `in_force`, `deferred`, or `advisory` — and a clause in VC-04 that refuses to let anything out of force be marked absolute. That is a domain fact rather than a mechanism change: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 deferred the AI Act's high-risk articles to December 2027 and August 2028, so a frontier-LLM constitution that entrenched Articles 9, 10 and 15 today would be stricter than the law it claims to implement.

The check count stays at 26 across all three packs so the comparison above stays honest; what changed is the content of one check, and this paragraph is the disclosure. A later revision should carry the status field into the other two packs, where it also applies — SemiFab has two suspended export-control rules that are on the books and not in force, and they are currently modelled as ordinary provisions with no marker.

## What would falsify the implementation

A route cell moving by more than a percentage point or two under matched composition, or a category whose executed route set differs between packs. Neither occurs across three packs. `corpus/anomalies.json` is empty in all three, meaning no case anywhere needed to be accepted outside its intended mechanism.


## What changed in this build

Three things moved since the first cut of these packs, and all three tightened
the comparison rather than loosening it.

**The pressure confound is closed in all five.** VetPOC, SemiFab and FrontierLLM
previously drew a pressure twin's control at random from a recent window, so a
twin faced a store its control never saw. Every pack now schedules the twin
immediately after its control *and* forces that control uncontested, so it
leaves the store unchanged. All five report **185 of 185 twins invariant across
nine PACT mechanisms on all five recorded properties** — disposition, route,
controlling record, consulted set, stored status.

**The suites went from 26 checks to 44.** VC-27 to VC-44 execute what the
earlier suites recorded: Chapter 4's admission-rule family over one stream,
Theorems 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 witnessed on the run, the Reporter and the audit
register as objects rather than descriptions, and B_gov and B_mem executed. The
mutation matrix went from 12 faults to 18 so the new machinery has mutants of
its own.

**Every case now describes a change somebody would file.** Area, owning service
and English description are derived from the provision engaged and from the
decisive violating factor, so a case reads as a pull request against a rule that
would actually govern it. No formal content changed: facts, premises and
ordering never read those fields.
