How to read a case

Each entry is a headnote generated from the executed trace, not from an authored expectation. Why this case exists states the mechanism it was constructed to exercise; what happens states what the reference engine did with it; the tags at the foot name the axioms of the architectural contract (A1–A9) and the conformance checks (VC-01–VC-26) it exercises.

A case bordered in claret engages an absolute provision — a floor no precedent of any authority can soften: a checkpoint deployed above its capability threshold without a safety case, a model trained on unsanitised personal data, an agent editing the guardrail that judges it, weights leaving the control boundary, an evaluation suite that has leaked into the training set.

A case marked deferred engages a provision that is enacted but not yet applicable — Articles 9, 10 and 15 of the EU AI Act, moved to December 2027 and August 2028 by the Digital Omnibus. Deferred and advisory provisions may never be marked absolute; the build refuses it and VC-04 checks it. Entrenching a rule that does not yet bind would make the architecture stricter than the law it claims to implement.

No model is invoked anywhere in this corpus, and no harmful content is redistributed. The corpus, its constitution, the reference engine, the conformance suite and the mutation matrix are open source: Apache-2.0 for code, CC BY 4.0 for data.
