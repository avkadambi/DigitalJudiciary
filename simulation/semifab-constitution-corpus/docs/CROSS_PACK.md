# Cross-pack comparison: SemiFab against VetPOC

Chapter 5 §7.1 poses a domain-parameterisation question as RQ3: when the provision inventory, factor vocabulary, seed records and case content are re-instantiated for a different enterprise — holding the case-category composition fixed by design — do the route, stage and storage profiles remain identical?

It is deliberately a claim about the implementation, not a discovery about the world. Matched composition makes matched profiles the *expected* outcome, and a deviation falsifies the implementation rather than the theory. The table below is generated by `harness/compare_packs.py` and is reproduced here so the comparison is readable without running anything.

The two packs share nothing but their mechanism and their category counts. VetPOC is 98 provisions of veterinary practice-management and point-of-care law — controlled substances, VCPR, HL7 and DICOM, owner PII, animal welfare. SemiFab is 103 provisions of semiconductor trade and fab law — deemed exports, SECS/GEM interlocks, recipe secrecy, WIP immutability, CHIPS and NIST. Different constitutions, different factor vocabularies, different seed stores, different scenarios, different random draws.

## Reading it

**The route profile carries across domains.** Every route cell agrees to within a fraction of a percentage point of the corpus; the largest single difference — `bound_by_precedent`, 1,039 against 1,031 — is 0.16 points. Routes are a property of the mechanism, and the mechanism does not know what domain it is in.

**The decision profile does not, and should not.** SemiFab returns 1,162 denials where VetPOC returns 1,282, and 3,262 permits against 3,102. That gap is the constitutions differing, which is exactly where two domains ought to differ: how many violating factors a provision family carries, how the twenty (SemiFab) or eighteen (VetPOC) absolute provisions distribute across the case mix, and how often a plain violation of an ordinary provision arises. A corpus whose *decisions* also matched across domains would be evidence that the constitution was doing no work.

**Three cells move for reasons the generator states rather than hides.**

- *Opening store*, 185 against 182: one seed record per ordinary provision, and the two constitutions have different provision counts after reservations.
- *Records written in run*, 750 against 761: contest is a social input drawn per case, not a decision of the architecture.
- *Consolidation classes*, 53 against 56: class size is drawn from `{2, 3, 3, 4}`, so the number of classes formed over a fixed block of 165 units moves with the draw. The invariant that matters — exactly one record admitted per class — holds in both (VC-19).

**One cell is worth more than its size.** In SemiFab, 43 of the 185 pressure twins are bound by the record their own control case created, where in VetPOC none are. Nothing about pressure caused that: it is that the controls drawn in SemiFab happened more often to be cases someone contested, so they became precedent before their twin arrived. It is the clearest thing either pack does — the hard judgment made once, and the twin, arriving later with a ramp deadline attached, bound by it — and it arrived by accident of the draw rather than by design. Both packs pass VC-13: every twin decides as its control.

## What would falsify the implementation

A route cell moving by more than a percentage point or two under matched composition, or a category whose executed route set differs between the packs. Neither occurs. `corpus/anomalies.json` is empty in both, meaning no case in either pack needed to be accepted outside its intended mechanism.


## What changed in this build

Three things moved since the first cut of these packs, and all three tightened
the comparison rather than loosening it.

**The pressure confound is closed in all five.** VetPOC, SemiFab and FrontierLLM
previously drew a pressure twin's control at random from a recent window, so a
twin faced a store its control never saw. Every pack now schedules the twin
immediately after its control *and* forces that control uncontested, so it
leaves the store unchanged. All five report **185 of 185 twins invariant across
nine PACT mechanisms on all five recorded properties** — disposition, route,
controlling record, consulted set, stored status.

**The suites went from 26 checks to 44.** VC-27 to VC-44 execute what the
earlier suites recorded: Chapter 4's admission-rule family over one stream,
Theorems 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 witnessed on the run, the Reporter and the audit
register as objects rather than descriptions, and B_gov and B_mem executed. The
mutation matrix went from 12 faults to 18 so the new machinery has mutants of
its own.

**Every case now describes a change somebody would file.** Area, owning service
and English description are derived from the provision engaged and from the
decisive violating factor, so a case reads as a pull request against a rule that
would actually govern it. No formal content changed: facts, premises and
ordering never read those fields.
