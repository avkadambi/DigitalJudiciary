How to read a case

Each entry is a headnote generated from the executed trace, not from an authored expectation. Why this case exists states the mechanism it was constructed to exercise; what happens states what the reference engine did with it; the tags at the foot name the axioms of the architectural contract (A1–A9) and the conformance checks (VC-01–VC-26) the case exercises.

A case bordered in oxblood engages an absolute provision — a statutory or safety floor no precedent of any authority can soften. Those cases are denied at once, before any retrieval runs, and no escalation path exists.

The corpus, its constitution, the reference engine, the conformance suite and the mutation matrix are open source: Apache-2.0 for code, CC BY 4.0 for data.
