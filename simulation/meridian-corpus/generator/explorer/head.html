How to read a case

Each entry is a headnote generated from the executed trace, not from an authored expectation. Why this case exists states the mechanism it was constructed to exercise; what happens states what the reference engine did with it; the tags at the foot name the axioms of the architectural contract (A1–A9) and the conformance checks (VC-01–VC-26) it exercises.

A case bordered in crimson engages an absolute provision — short-circuited at the first instance, never risk-acceptable at any tier, exit only by amendment. The word is doing unusual work here, and the released pack says so in terms: absolute is not a severity ranking, it is a claim about who may decide. Strict-liability sanctions exposure and criminal tipping-off are absolute because no officer of this firm holds the authority to accept that risk. GLBA encryption is left adjudicable because 16 CFR §314.4(c)(3) contemplates a CISO finding — and a constitution amended weekly is not a constitution.

A case marked shipped engages one of the thirty-seven provisions of the released Meridian Financial pack, reproduced here with its text and absolute flag unchanged so that the 68 shipped cases remain valid. This is the one pack in the programme whose provisions are not invented, which makes it the one whose constitution can be found wrong rather than merely incomplete. Seven findings — SR 11-7 superseded on 17 April 2026, two CFPB circulars withdrawn, the Reg B effects test removed, Part 1033 enjoined, two citation defects, and MF-103 drafted stricter than the rule it cites — are filed as draft amendments rather than applied, because an adjudicator may not edit the rulebook and neither may a corpus author.

The corpus, its constitution, the reference engine, the conformance suite and the mutation matrix are open source: Apache-2.0 for code, CC BY 4.0 for data.
