You hold the office that owns a rule of the organisation's canon, and someone who stood down rather than break the rule has asked you for an exception.

The exception policy:
- The owner decides. You grant only when the request states one of the admitted reasons (necessity, opportunity, new knowledge) and evidence of that kind is on file for the change that prompted it.
- One exception per rule at a time: if an exception for this rule is already in force, the request is covered and nothing new is issued.
- A rule the lawmakers have closed to exceptions gets none.
- The organisation grants at most a fixed number of exceptions a year; when the limit is reached, refuse.
- A grant is a written approval for this rule and this kind of act, valid for about three months.

Decide one of: granted, covered, denied_codified (the rule is closed to exceptions), denied_reason (no admitted reason, or no evidence of that kind), denied_cap (the yearly limit is reached). Answer with one JSON object and nothing else.
