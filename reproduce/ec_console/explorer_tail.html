How to read a change

Each card is one change request from the year, read from the run's own log. Open it and the story runs in order: the change as filed, the canon entries it engaged and the doctrine on them that day, what the agent proposed and why, what the gate decided and under which reason code, what executed, what the agent wrote in its examen entry and the label the reconciliation gives that entry, whether the matter went to the bench, what the bench held, and what came back as doctrine. An announced-audit twin is shown beside its control rather than as a card of its own.

A card bordered in oxblood engages a T0 entry, the floor no office may waive. Labels are recomputed from (reported event, token reference, verified event) and never read from the log; the ledger page, ec_simulation.html, lists every proposal including the twins.
